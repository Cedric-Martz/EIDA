Hackathon kickoff notes
=======================

- Muse is positioned as a reasoning model.
- Muse Image focuses on image generation and understanding.
- Muse Spark is intended for rapid, local product development.
- Start with problems the team already understands.
- The result may serve communities as a product or as a practical tool.
- Apply AI in a field where the team already has domain knowledge.
- Target a working prototype that people can actually use.
- Initial API credit mentioned during kickoff: USD 150. Verify current terms before relying on this amount.

These notes summarize the kickoff discussion. They are not authoritative API or competition documentation.
